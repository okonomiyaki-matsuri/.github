# Security Policy

Materia Inc. (株式会社Materia) takes the security of its products and its customers' data seriously. This policy applies to every repository in this GitHub organization and to the services listed under **Scope**.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **info@themateria.jp** with the subject line `[Security] <short description>`.

Please do **not** open a public GitHub issue for security reports.

Include as much of the following as you can:

- The affected service, URL, or repository
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- The impact you believe the issue has
- Your name or handle, if you would like to be credited

Reports are accepted in English or Japanese.

## What to expect

- We will acknowledge your report within 5 business days.
- We will keep you informed while we investigate and remediate.
- We ask for a reasonable period to fix the issue before it is disclosed publicly, and we will coordinate the timing of any disclosure with you.

We do not currently run a bug bounty program and do not offer monetary rewards. We are glad to credit reporters who wish to be named.

## Scope

In scope:

- `*.themateria.jp`
- `*.themateria.net`
- Source code in this organization's repositories

Out of scope:

- Denial-of-service or other volumetric testing
- Social engineering, phishing, or physical attacks against our staff or customers
- Issues in third-party services we use (please report those to the vendor)
- Automated scanner output without a demonstrated impact

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who act in good faith: who avoid privacy violations, data destruction, and service disruption; who access data only to the extent needed to demonstrate the issue; and who report to us promptly and give us the chance to fix the issue before disclosing it.

## Supported versions

Materia is a hosted service. Only the currently deployed version of each service is supported; there are no self-hosted releases.
